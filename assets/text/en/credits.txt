# EU SIMULATOR
The internship of your life

An unofficial parody game.
No affiliation with the European Union,
its institutions, or Nintendo.

# Design and programming
The EU Simulator contributors
with l'Ordinatrice (AI assistant)

# Engine
C + libnds, BlocksDS (zlib, MIT, CC0)
Tested in melonDS

# Music
Tracks generated with BeepLoop
Ode to Joy: L. van Beethoven (public domain)
Sound effects: custom PSG synthesis
