# Open-source art used in EU Simulator

Menu icons for bag, team, report and settings: Mona © 2025 Monad ABXY.
Original font and OFL 1.1 license: mona/. See mona/CREDITS.md for the pinned source
and the glyph rasterization used by tools/mkmona.py.

Trees, bushes, rocks, stumps and flowers on the maps are cut from the Tuxemon tilesets
(https://github.com/Tuxemon/Tuxemon, `mods/tuxemon/gfx/tilesets/core_outdoor_nature.png`),
then recoloured (blue shadows, colour grade) by `tools/oss_art.py` and `tools/tiles.py`.

"Core Outdoor Nature" by rubberduck, based on "Updated Ground Tiles" by rubberduck (original tiles by Buch,
commissioned by luke83, with changes by luke83 and Past the Future, and tiles by George_), "Sand n Water"
by luke83 based on "Kyrodian Legends Overworld Tileset" by Midi, and "Grass, Sand, Snow" by PastTheFuture.

Because of CC BY-SA, the modified tree/flower art in this game is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0 as well.
