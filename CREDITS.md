## Programming and design
The EU Simulator contributors, with l'Ordinatrice (AI assistant).
Prototype rapidly generated using **Claude Opus 5.5** and **GPT-6.1 Sol**.

## Libraries and tools
- **BlocksDS** and **libnds** (https://blocksds.skylyrac.net) — zlib, MIT and CC0 licences (BlocksDS's `licenses/` directory).
  Linked into the ROM; compatible with GPL v3.
- Nintendo DS networking: **DSWifi** (https://blocksds.skylyrac.net/dswifi/, MIT),
  with lwIP (BSD) and Mbed TLS (Apache 2.0); notices in `assets/oss/dswifi/`.
- Multiplayer server: **ws** (https://github.com/websockets/ws, MIT);
  notice in `docs/licenses/ws-LICENSE.txt`, installed via npm.
- Build tools: Python, Pillow, NumPy, fontTools, ffmpeg (not distributed with the game).
- Tested with melonDS (not distributed).
- Browser page: **EmulatorJS 4.2.3** (https://github.com/EmulatorJS/EmulatorJS, GPL v3),
  **DeSmuME** core (https://github.com/libretro/desmume, GPL v2); loaded on demand from
  the EmulatorJS CDN, not included in the repository. DS case drawn in CSS by the contributors.

## Music
- 48 tracks (`assets/music_ogg/`) generated with [**BeepLoop**](beeploop.fabs.lu) from the project's instructions, released by the contributors under CC BY-SA 4.0.
- *Ode to Joy*: Ludwig van Beethoven (public domain). No protected recording or arrangement is used
  (in particular, not Herbert von Karajan's arrangement of the European anthem).
- Sound effects: PSG synthesis generated by the game code.

## Graphics
- Bag, team, report, settings and flee (two characters) menu icons: **Mona**, © 2025 Monad ABXY
  ([source](https://github.com/MonadABXY/mona-font), revision 07029e3c1f4e3491b0ed707b27953d1ace78d9b2).
  Glyphs rendered on their original grid, then adapted to RGB15 and scaled up ×2 for the DS.
  Source font under SIL OFL 1.1; source, licence and details in `assets/oss/mona/`.
- **SerenityOS** game controller emoji (website controller icon, outside this folder): the SerenityOS developers,
  [source](https://github.com/SerenityOS/serenity/blob/master/Base/res/emoji/U%2B1F3AE.png),
  under BSD 2-Clause (an exception to the content licence); text in `docs/licenses/LICENSE-SERENITY.txt`.
- Tiles, buildings, map characters, Euromons, interface: procedural pixel art (`tools/`), CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Cutscene illustrations, backgrounds and portraits: AI-generated (OpenAI gpt-image), retouched and
  pixelated with `tools/`, released under CC BY-SA 4.0. Prompts: `docs/eusim_images.json`.
- **Trees, bushes, rocks, flowers**: extracted from the **Tuxemon** project's “Core Outdoor Nature”
  (https://github.com/Tuxemon/Tuxemon, file `mods/tuxemon/gfx/tilesets/core_outdoor_nature.png`),
  recoloured (`tools/oss_art.py`). Authors: rubberduck, based on “Updated Ground Tiles” (rubberduck,
  original tiles by Buch commissioned by luke83, modifications by luke83 and Past the Future, tiles by
  George_), “Sand n Water” (luke83, based on Midi's “Kyrodian Legends Overworld Tileset”) and
  “Grass, Sand, Snow” (PastTheFuture, based on Midi). Licences: CC BY-SA 4.0, CC BY 3.0, CC BY 4.0
  (see Tuxemon's `ATTRIBUTIONS.md`). Modified version: CC BY-SA 4.0. Details: `assets/oss/CREDITS.md`.
- Map of Europe: **Natural Earth** (public domain).
