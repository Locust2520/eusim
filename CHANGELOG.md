# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-23

- **English is now the game's source language.** All text is written in English and translated into the 23 other EU languages, French included. Existing translations are unchanged. On the name keyboard, QWERTY is now the default; it stays AZERTY in French.
- **Larger cities, with local references explained.** Brussels (Grand-Place), Luxembourg (Ville-Haute, Bock casemates), Frankfurt (Römerberg, Paulskirche, Stock Exchange, the Main, Sachsenhausen, Money Museum), Strasbourg (Grande Île, Petite France, Council of Europe), Athens (Syntagma, Anafiotika, Monastiraki, Acropolis Museum, Zappeion, Panathenaic Stadium), Piraeus (passenger port, reached by Line 1), London (British Museum, ferry terminal), Budapest (Széchenyi baths, Chain Bridge, Buda) and Paris (Quai d'Orsay, the Seine, Notre-Dame), plus additions in Antwerp, Warsaw, Rome, Lisbon and on routes 1 and 2.
- **The ECB's secret studio.** In Frankfurt, a door hidden behind Europa's portrait leads to the studio designing the next euro banknotes (the ten finalist designs of 2026). After a feel-look-tilt test, Eleni engraves your portrait on a SPECIMEN note: take a photo with the DSi camera (DSi, 3DS), draw it with the stylus, or use your official portrait. Choose its theme and value. The note is kept in your save, can be viewed from the Bag, and appears at the end of the game.
- **Eleven new mini-games:** feel-look-tilt, apple wine from the Bembel, the changing of the guard, the Athens kiosk, loading a container ship in Piraeus, the Grand-Place flower carpet, the storks of Alsace, post-Brexit customs, chess in the Széchenyi baths, the Schuman Declaration and the Bock casemates.
- **The jury's twelve postcards:** the motifs of the future banknotes are hidden across Europe. Collect them all to unlock a gold edition of your note.
- Saves made with earlier versions still load. The banknote is stored in an optional block after the save data, on SD cards, cartridges and in the browser (export and import included).
- Memorials at the Grossmarkthalle (Frankfurt) and on the Danube bank (Budapest) are presented soberly.
- All new text is translated into the 23 languages. The stamp mini-game, wireless debate messages and the save-error message, previously English-only outside French, are now translated too.
- Fixed: the location banner was sized for the English map name, so longer translated names were squeezed or cut.
- Fixed: chapter cards and several messages (Eurodex counts, move learning, report sent) were cut short or showed garbage characters in Greek and Bulgarian.

## 1.0.2 — 2026-10-21

- After evolution, a Euromon with four moves now offers to forget a move to learn the new form's move at its current level. This fixes Cryptou evolving into MiCA at level 30 without being offered Enquête OLAF after learning Spread.
- Players can decline or cancel the replacement. Moves already known keep their remaining PP.

This fix applies to future evolutions; it does not restore moves missed by Euromons that have already evolved.
